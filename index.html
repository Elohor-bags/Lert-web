We never say “scam” on one weak signal.

A fraud tool that wrongly accuses is a liability. Every verdict passes a four-tier evidence gate, so users can trust it and regulators can defend it.

1 · Verified consistent

Registry or identity match confirmed, no tampering, no anomalies. “No Inconsistencies Found,” with the verified facts.

2 · Unverified, sparse

Too little public footprint, but no contradictions. “Proceed With Caution,” with the unconfirmed claims.

3 · Inconsistency flagged

Objective anomalies below the fraud threshold. Every discrepancy listed. No “Scam” label.

4 · High-confidence fraud

At least two independent forensic proofs, or three corroborated threat-feed hits. Exact criteria and cited evidence shown.
